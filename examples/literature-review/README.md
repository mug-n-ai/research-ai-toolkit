# What did the sodium detection measure?

Use the [literature-review skill](../../skills/literature-review/SKILL.md) to check what Charbonneau, Brown, Noyes and Gilliland measured when they looked for sodium in the atmosphere of HD 209458 b. The example uses the authors' arXiv v1 manuscript, which contains the full text, tables and all eight figures. [Full paper with figures](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544v1) · [Journal article, 2002](https://doi.org/10.1086/338770)

## Input

> Read the full paper and inspect its figures. Then check this sentence for a review of early transmission spectroscopy: “Charbonneau et al. (2002) detected sodium in HD 209458 b and measured its atmospheric abundance.” Which part does the paper support, and what wording would you use in the review?

## Example output

**What was measured.** The authors used Hubble/STIS spectra to compare the transit in a narrow band covering the sodium doublet (588.7–589.9 nm) with the simultaneous light curves in the neighbouring bands. Four transits were observed and three contributed to the analysis. The transit was deeper in the narrow band by (2.32 ± 0.57) × 10⁻⁴ in relative flux, with a 1σ uncertainty on the mean. Figure 4 shows the narrow-band signal in time. Its solid curve is a model scaled to the observed offset. [Section 2.2, equation 5](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544v1#page=6) · [Table 1](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544v1#page=15) · [Figure 4](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544v1#page=20)

**What can be inferred.** The authors interpreted the extra absorption as evidence for sodium in the atmosphere. The signal was weaker than their reference model predicted. Both higher clouds and a lower abundance of atomic sodium can weaken the feature in the models, so these data leave the abundance undetermined. Figure 8 gives predictions for how broader wavelength coverage could help distinguish those explanations. [Sections 4.2 and 4.3](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544v1#page=9) · [Figure 8](https://arxiv.org/pdf/astro-ph/0111544v1#page=26)

**Suggested wording.** “Charbonneau et al. (2002) found that the transit of HD 209458 b was deeper around the sodium doublet than in the adjacent bands and interpreted the additional absorption as sodium in the planet's atmosphere.” [Charbonneau et al. (2002)](https://doi.org/10.1086/338770)

**Review record.** Full arXiv v1 manuscript read, including Tables 1 and 2 and Figures 1–8 with captions. The check covers what this paper established with its own data. If your assistant cannot open the figures, give it the PDF and ask it to record which figures it could inspect.
